## Priority Issues

**[P0] Primary CTA has no fallback path.**
- Why it matters: both "START A CONVERSATION →" and the footer "EMAIL" link are `mailto:hello@ginesh.dev` with no visible plaintext address anywhere on the page (confirmed via grep - the address exists only inside two `href` attributes). This is the entire purpose of the site; on a corporate/recruiter machine with no default mail client, the click does nothing and there's no recovery.
- Fix: show the plaintext address next to or instead of the mailto link, add a copy-to-clipboard affordance.
- Suggested command: `/impeccable clarify`

**[P1] Scene 05 breaks its own chunking rule at its most important moment.**
- Why it matters: the LogiqLead/LogiqAIAssist breakdown renders Problem/Approach/Architecture/Product/Result as 5 simultaneous columns (`Products.css` `.breakdown-grid`), violating the site's own consistently-applied ≤4-items-per-group rule (Scene 10/Focus correctly uses 4). This is also the single densest moment on an otherwise very disciplined site, right where a skimming CTO needs the clearest read.
- Fix: drop to 4 groups (fold Approach into Problem, or Architecture into Product) or stack 2+3 across two rows.
- Suggested command: `/impeccable layout`

**[P1] Product evidence is abstract and inconsistently applied.**
- Why it matters: the brief explicitly calls for "a large, high-quality product interface" for LogiqLead; what's shipped is a low-fidelity gray-bar wireframe, visually interchangeable with any SaaS mockup. LogiqAIAssist gets no visual at all. This undercuts "evidence not decoration" for the site's stated centerpiece.
- Fix: invest in one higher-fidelity, recognizably LogiqLead-specific interface visual; give LogiqAIAssist visual parity or an intentionally different treatment that still reads as evidence.
- Suggested command: `/impeccable polish`

**[P2] Five CSS transitions animate layout-triggering properties instead of transform/opacity.**
- Why it matters: mechanically confirmed by the detector - `CustomCursor.css:16` (`width, height, margin`), `Hero.css:52` (`width`), `ProblemSolving.css:12`, `Products.css:165`, `Timeline.css:12` (all `padding-left`). These force layout recalculation on every frame instead of running on the compositor thread, a real (if currently invisible) performance cost, and a direct violation of the brief's own "Avoid layout-triggering animations" performance rule.
- Fix: rewrite each to animate `transform`/`clip-path` equivalents.
- Suggested command: `/impeccable optimize`

**[P2] No focus-visible styling anywhere in the codebase.**
- Why it matters: grep across `styles/` and `components/` found zero `:focus`/`:focus-visible` rules; the site relies entirely on default UA focus rings, despite the brief explicitly listing "Proper focus states" as a requirement. A site this typographically considered has an easy opportunity to make keyboard focus feel as premium as the existing hover states.
- Fix: add a custom `:focus-visible` treatment using the existing teal accent on nav links, both CTAs, and case-study rows.
- Suggested command: `/impeccable harden`
